Phase 1 · Design foundation

EduRise Global design system

The colours, type, spacing and components every page will be built from. Agreeing on these first keeps all eight phases consistent, and lets later changes happen in one place.

TrustworthyCalm navy and royal blue from the logo, real people, no exaggerated claims.
ClearLarge readable type, one main action per section, fees and dates easy to scan.
Mobile firstMost visitors arrive from Facebook on a phone. Every component works at 375px.

The official mortarboard logo (used in the PDF profile, business cards and Facebook posts) is the only logo. The green "G" logo from the old website and the gold paper-plane variant are not used.

Colour

Royal blue and navy come from the logo. Yellow is the highlight used in EduRise's posts. Green is reserved for the main action, usually WhatsApp. Red appears only when a deadline is close. Click a swatch to copy its value. The badge shows whether its text pairing passes the WCAG AA contrast check.

Spacing and layout

A 4px-based scale keeps gaps consistent. Content is never wider than 1180px, with a 20px side margin on desktop and 16px on phones.

Icons

One outline style at a 2px stroke, stored as a single icon set in the page. Click an icon to copy the code that uses it.

Imagery

Real people and real places, never collages. Students should look like EduRise's audience: young South Asian students and families. Each frame below is a reusable placeholder that becomes a photo once the image is added.

Sources we use

Free-licence libraries such as Unsplash and Pexels, plus EduRise's own photos of its team, office and students (with consent). Each photo is downloaded, compressed to WebP and stored in assets/img/.

What we avoid

Images copied from Google search (usually copyrighted), AI collages with distorted text, university logos and photos without permission, and stock faces presented as real students.

Next step for photos: to download from Unsplash or Pexels, I need your computer connected through the Claude desktop app. You can also attach photos here.
